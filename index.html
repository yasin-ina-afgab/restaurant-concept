Our story

Good things come to those who cook for others.

Damal began in Hargeisa with a simple idea: cook the food our families grew up on, generously and with care. Today the same recipes travel with our community — to the busy streets of East London, and soon, to new cities.

“Damal” is the great shade tree where people gather. That's what we want every branch to be.
